omarchy-linux-troubleshooter is an evidence-first troubleshooting skill for one specific machine: the ASUS Vivobook S 14 OLED M5406WA (Ryzen AI 9 365, Radeon 880M, MT7922) on Omarchy/Arch/Hyprland.

It makes the agent diagnose before changing anything, tag every claim as confirmed, likely or speculation, make one change at a time, and prove the fix with a before/after check. It ships with a read-only, redacted audit script (scripts/audit.sh) and on-demand checklists for power, GPU, OLED/HDR, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth, suspend, storage and security.

Other hardware: treat the checklists as examples, not guarantees.
